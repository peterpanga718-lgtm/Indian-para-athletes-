About para-sports

What are para-athletes?

Para-athletes are athletes with eligible impairments who compete in adapted or classified sporting events. They train with the same discipline and intensity as elite sportspersons, often under more demanding conditions. Their achievements represent not just personal excellence, but a broader message of inclusion and equal opportunity.
