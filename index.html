About para-sports

What are para-athletes?

Para-athletes are athletes with eligible impairments who compete in adapted sports events under international rules. Their achievements are not only remarkable in terms of performance, but also in courage, discipline, and the challenge of pushing beyond barriers.

🏆Strong competition at national and global levels
🤝Inclusion through athletic excellence and visibility
💡Inspiring the next generation of sport leaders
🌍Building global recognition for Indian sport
