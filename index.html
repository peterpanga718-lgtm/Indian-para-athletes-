About para-sports

What are para-athletes?

Para-athletes are sportspeople with eligible impairments who compete in adapted or classified sporting events. Para-sport includes many disciplines, such as shooting, athletics, swimming, badminton and table tennis.

Classification helps group athletes fairly according to how an impairment affects the requirements of a particular sport.

Our purpose

Sport can open doors.

These stories celebrate training, teamwork, skill and achievement—not just medals. Para-athletes help audiences recognise the importance of access, inclusion and equal opportunities in sport.
